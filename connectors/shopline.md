# SHOPLINE

## What it unlocks

Your SHOPLINE store in the same answer as your ads, your payment processor, and your accounting file. Ask what is selling, what is sitting in inventory, and which customers came back, without opening the admin panel.

SHOPLINE is most useful paired with a paid-media connector and an accounting connector: one question then spans demand, stock, and cash.

## Before you connect

- A SHOPLINE store where you have admin access.
- About 2 minutes.

## How to connect

1. Open your CorpusIQ dashboard and click Connectors.
2. Find SHOPLINE and click Connect.
3. Sign in to SHOPLINE as the store admin if you are not already signed in.
4. Review the read-only permissions and approve them.

The SHOPLINE card changes from gray to Connected. If you run several stores, connect each one separately so answers stay scoped to the right store.

## What CorpusIQ can see

- Orders: order volume, values, and status.
- Products: catalogue, variants, and pricing.
- Inventory: stock levels by product and variant.
- Customers: customer records and purchase history.

Read-only. CorpusIQ does not edit products, change prices, fulfil orders, or message customers.

## Questions you can ask

- "Which SHOPLINE products sold most last month?"
- "What is out of stock right now that sold last week?"
- "Which customers ordered more than once this quarter?"
- "Compare SHOPLINE revenue with Google Ads spend for the same period."

## Troubleshooting

- Store not found: the SHOPLINE account you authorized does not have admin access to the store. Sign in with the store admin account and reconnect.
- Inventory numbers look stale: stock levels update as SHOPLINE recalculates them; reconnect if a full day passes without changes.
- Wrong store connected: disconnect SHOPLINE in the dashboard, switch stores in SHOPLINE, then reconnect.
