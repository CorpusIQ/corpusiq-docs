# How to Get the Same Answer From Every AI

You ask ChatGPT about your monthly revenue. It gives you one number.

You ask Claude the same question. It gives you a different number.

Both sound confident. Both cite plausible logic. Neither has access to your actual Stripe account, your actual Shopify orders, or your actual QuickBooks ledger.

This is the consistency problem. Every AI is smart. No AI can see your business data. So every AI guesses.

## The Real Problem

It is not that AI is bad at math. It is that your business data lives in forty different tools and no AI has access to any of them.

Your Stripe knows your actual revenue. Your Shopify knows your actual orders. Your QuickBooks knows your actual margins. Your Meta Ads knows your actual spend.

But they do not talk to each other. And they certainly do not talk to your AI.

So you become the bridge. You export from Stripe. Export from Shopify. Paste into a spreadsheet. Format. Calculate. Hope nothing is missing. Then you tell your AI what you found.

By the time you have an answer, you could have just done the math yourself.
