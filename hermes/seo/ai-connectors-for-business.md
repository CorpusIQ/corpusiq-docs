# AI Connectors for Business: Connect ChatGPT to Shopify, QuickBooks, Stripe

Your AI cannot answer basic questions about your business. Not because it is not smart enough. Because it cannot reach your data.

AI connectors solve this. They create a read-only bridge between your business tools and the AI you already use. Ask a question. Get a verified answer with every source cited.

## What AI Connectors Do

An AI connector authenticates to one business tool. Shopify. QuickBooks. Stripe. HubSpot. GA4. Using read-only OAuth. The AI can query data. It cannot modify anything.

When you ask ChatGPT "what was revenue last month," the connectors query your actual tools:
- Shopify connector returns order data
- Stripe connector returns payment data
- QuickBooks connector returns financial data

The AI reconciles the numbers and returns one answer. With the breakdown. With source citations. Same answer in Claude. Same answer in Perplexity.

## The Connectors That Matter Most

**Shopify connector**: Orders, products, customers, inventory. Every metric your store generates.

**QuickBooks connector**: Chart of accounts, transactions, profit and loss, balance sheet. Your financial truth.

**Stripe connector**: Payments, subscriptions, invoices, disputes. The money side of your business.

**HubSpot connector**: Contacts, deals, companies, tickets. Your customer relationships.

**GA4 connector**: Traffic, conversions, user behavior, campaign performance. Your marketing reality.

**Meta Ads connector**: Campaigns, ad sets, ads, performance data. Your paid acquisition truth.

**Google Ads connector**: Campaigns, keywords, quality scores, conversion data. Your search marketing.

**Klaviyo connector**: Campaigns, flows, segments, metrics. Your email marketing performance.

## How They Work Together

The power is not in any single connector. It is in the cross-source queries.

Ask "which marketing channel has the highest ROAS" and the system queries Meta Ads, Google Ads, Shopify (for actual sales), and GA4 (for attribution). Returns one answer with the blended and per-channel numbers. All sourced.

Ask "which customers are at risk of churning" and the system queries Stripe (payment failures), HubSpot (low engagement), and Intercom (support ticket volume). Returns a ranked list with the evidence.

Ask "what is our cash runway" and the system queries QuickBooks (current balance, burn rate), Stripe (upcoming payments), and your bank (available credit). Returns actual numbers with projections.

## Security and Privacy

Every connector uses read-only OAuth. The AI can see your data but never change it. No orders can be modified. No financials can be edited. No customer records can be altered.

Each connector authenticates independently. Shopify has its own token. QuickBooks has its own. Stripe has its own. No shared credentials. No master key.

Data is fetched for each query and discarded. Nothing is stored between questions. Nothing is used for AI training. The connectors are ephemeral.

## Getting Started

The first three connectors are the most important: Shopify, QuickBooks, Stripe. Connect those and you can answer 80% of your business questions.

Each connection takes minutes. Authorize read-only access. Ask your first question. Verify against the source tool. The numbers should match.

From there, add connectors as you need them. Marketing. CRM. Analytics. Each one makes your AI smarter about your actual business.

## The Difference

Without connectors: ChatGPT guesses about your business based on training data. Plausible numbers. Not your numbers.

With connectors: ChatGPT returns your actual data. From your actual tools. With your actual metrics. Verified. Source-cited. Consistent across every AI you use.

This is not a better AI model. It is better AI infrastructure.
