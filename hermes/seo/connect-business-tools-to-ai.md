# Connect Your Business Tools to AI

Your AI is smart. It can write code. Draft emails. Analyze documents.

But it cannot tell you your June revenue. Or your actual margin. Or which ad campaign drove sales this month.

Not because it is not smart enough. Because it has no connection to your tools.

CorpusIQ fixes this.

## How It Works

You connect your business tools once. Read-only OAuth. Five minutes.

Then every AI you use — ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Slack — inherits the connections.

Ask a question. The intelligence layer routes to your Shopify for orders, Stripe for payments, QuickBooks for margin, Meta Ads for spend. It validates the numbers against each other. It returns a source-cited answer.

Same question. Same data. Same answer. Every AI. Every time.

## What You Can Connect

Shopify. Stripe. QuickBooks. Google Analytics. Meta Ads. HubSpot. Klaviyo. Gmail. Google Drive. And thirty more.

Read-only on every connection. No write access. Direct MCP does not retain raw customer files or full connector response payloads; scoped operational logs may be retained for up to 30 days. OAuth credentials are encrypted and retained while the connection is active.

Every answer cites its exact source — order number, transaction ID, campaign name. You can verify it instead of trusting it blind.

Monday morning. Instead of logging into six dashboards and building a report by hand, you open ChatGPT and ask: "How did we do last week?"

Revenue from Stripe. Orders from Shopify. Margin from QuickBooks. Ad spend from Meta. Traffic from GA4.

One answer. Source-cited. Thirty seconds.

Your AI stops guessing. It starts answering.
