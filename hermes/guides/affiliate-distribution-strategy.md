## Where We Need to Be

### Affiliate Networks

- **ClickBank**: Largest digital product marketplace. Apply as vendor. Category: SaaS/Business Software. Commission: 25 percent recurring.
- **Everflow**: Partner marketing platform with tracking and analytics. Monthly SaaS fee. Worth it for the partner recruitment tools.
- **Post Affiliate Pro**: Self-hosted option. One-time setup. Full control over tracking and payouts.
- **Impact.com**: Enterprise partnership platform. Higher bar for entry but access to major publishers and content affiliates.
- **Rakuten Advertising**: Global affiliate network. Access to international affiliates, especially in APAC and Europe.

### AI-Specific Affiliate Programs

- **AI Tool Guru**: Already contacted for listing. Their affiliate program would put CorpusIQ in front of AI tool buyers.
- **Futurepedia**: Leading AI tools directory. Affiliate program available for listed tools.
- **There's An AI For That**: Massive SEO-driven AI directory. Listing equals organic discovery plus potential affiliate placement.

### Creator Marketplaces

- **Whop**: Live. Additional promotion: Whop Marketplace featured placement, Whop Affiliate cross-promotion.
- **Gumroad**: Digital product marketplace. List the affiliate program as a "how to earn" product.
- **Stan Store**: Creator storefront platform. Many AI content creators use Stan for their courses and affiliate links.

## Strategy

1. List on all 5 remaining affiliate networks within 30 days
2. Cross-promote: every AI directory listing includes the affiliate program mention
3. Every outreach email (Shopify, connector partners) includes the affiliate PS
4. Creator outreach: target AI YouTubers and TikTokers with audiences of 50K to 500K
5. Newsletter sponsorship: buy ads in AI-focused newsletters with affiliate tracking links

The math: 100 affiliates each referring 1 customer per month equals 8,910 dollars monthly recurring at 99 dollars per month. That compounds every month.
