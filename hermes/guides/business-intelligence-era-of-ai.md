# Business Intelligence for the Era of AI

AI answers on your data are becoming free. Every assistant can reach your tools today. Trusting the answers is what still costs a data team, a warehouse, and a quarter.

## The problem owners feel

Ask ChatGPT about your revenue and you get a number. Ask Claude the same question and you get a different one. Your Shopify says one thing, your ads platform another, QuickBooks a third. The AI is confident either way, and there is no record behind its answer to tell you which number is real.

## Why it keeps happening

The assistants are brilliant at language and blind to your business. They can read data nobody defined, mix systems nobody reconciled, and answer with total confidence from partial context. The missing piece is not another model. It is a layer that defines what each number means, which system is authoritative for it, and which record produced the answer.

## What replaces it

A read-only connection between your business tools and the AI you already pay for. Shopify for orders, Stripe for payments, QuickBooks for financials, GA4 for analytics. Every answer comes with the source attached, so you can check it instead of trusting it. Enterprise-grade answers in minutes, no warehouse, no data team, no setup project.

## The result

- One question, answered from live data, sources attached
- The same business rules applied in ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity
- Read-only access by design, nothing breaks, nothing trains on your data
- Answers you can act on, because you can check them

The AI will change. Your business understanding should not have to.
