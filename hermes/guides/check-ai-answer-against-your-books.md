# How to Check an AI Answer Against Your Books

The AI gives you a revenue number in seconds. The question is not whether the AI is smart. The question is whether you can check its work in less time than it took to ask.

## The habit

Every AI answer about your business deserves the same three checks:

1. **The source check.** Which system produced this number? If the answer cannot show the record, it is a guess with better vocabulary.
2. **The definition check.** What does "revenue" mean in this answer? Gross sales, net after refunds, bookings, or cash received? Two systems can both be right and still disagree.
3. **The date check.** As of when? A Monday answer built on Friday's sync is wrong by Sunday.

## Why this matters for operators

You are already acting on numbers. The report you built last quarter, the dashboard you trust, the spreadsheet you update by hand. The AI answer is the same category of artifact: a number with a story attached. The difference is the AI produces it in seconds, which means it can be wrong in seconds too, and spread before lunch.

## The fix

Ask through a read-only connection that attaches the source to every figure. Shopify for orders, Stripe for payments, QuickBooks for financials. The answer comes back with the record behind each number, so the three checks take thirty seconds instead of thirty minutes.

Enterprise-grade answers in minutes, from $29.95 a month, no warehouse and no data team.

## The result

- Every figure traces to a source record
- Discrepancies surface before they become decisions
- One answer, checkable, in every AI you already use
- Trust that compounds, because you verified it once and it kept checking out

You do not need to trust AI with your business. You need to be able to check it.
