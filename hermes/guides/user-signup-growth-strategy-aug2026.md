# User Signup Growth Strategy — August 2026

Research-backed tactics to increase CorpusIQ signups. Based on 15+ authoritative SaaS growth sources, validated August 2026.

## Priority 1: AI Visibility

AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) are the new top-of-funnel. Buyers ask "best AI tool for X" before visiting websites. G2 and Capterra reviews are the number 1 citation source for these AI recommendations.

**Today:** Claim G2 and Capterra profiles. List pricing (even ranges help — 400% listing-view increase). Add comparison tables to every product page.
**This week:** Reach out to 10 existing users for honest reviews.
**Impact:** AI-referred traffic converts 22% higher than organic.

## Priority 2: Interactive Demo

Embed a product walkthrough on the homepage. No signup required. 

**Tools:** Navattic, Arcade (free tier available), Supademo.
**Impact:** 25% lift in signup conversion. 2x better than static screenshots.

## Priority 3: 5-Email Onboarding Drip

Behavioral sequence triggered by signup actions, not just time:

| Day | Email | Trigger |
|-----|-------|---------|
| 0 | Welcome + first action | Immediately |
| 1 | Brand story + social proof | If no setup started |
| 3 | Primary offer | If not activated |
| 5 | Education / use-case | Contextual tutorial |
| 7 | Re-engagement | If no activity |

**Impact:** 90% more conversions vs single welcome email. Mailchimp's "api generic signup" segment is ready — just needs the sequence wired.

## Priority 4: Community

30 minutes daily in 3 communities where business owners discuss AI tools. Help first, mention CorpusIQ only when earned.

**Target communities:** r/SaaS, r/entrepreneur, AI agent Discord servers, LinkedIn groups.
**Impact:** $45 CAC, 7.1:1 LTV — highest ROI channel in 2026.

## Priority 5: Funnel Optimization

- 3-tier pricing with "Most Popular" badge
- Signup form: 3-4 fields maximum
- Page load under 2 seconds
- Customer logos above the fold on signup page
