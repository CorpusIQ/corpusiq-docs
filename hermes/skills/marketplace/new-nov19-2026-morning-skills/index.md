## Notes on this batch

- Two guides ship Green, six Yellow and six Orange. The Green pair is Cuellar Design Skills, where every sampled verdict passes and the pack is ten focused UX skills, and Prompt Master, a single skill with three clean verdicts. Four of the six Orange guides are Orange only because the repository declares no licence at all, which is a compliance fact rather than a scanner finding.
- The band this fire was flat: the largest publisher published here is 4,998 installs and the smallest is 1,013, so the batch was chosen on topic value and licence clarity rather than on size. The thin ones earn their place: Boristane's wide event logging is a single skill that 4,998 agents load, and Trussary is five Vietnamese copywriting skills with no counterpart in the catalog.
- Two repositories carry an install index far larger than their skill tree. `sylphxai/skills` indexes 95 listings against 16 skill directories, and `oldwinter/skills` indexes 96 against 21. Both guides say so up front and describe only what can be reviewed in the repository.
- `lawve-ai/awesome-legal-skills` is the largest tree here: 281 skill directories across 3,189 files, curated per-author legal playbooks from contract review and privacy policy work to mediation and sanctions analysis. Its licence field reports NOASSERTION while the README states CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, so it is documented as source-available with the non-commercial restriction called out in four places.
- `stellarlinkco/myclaude` mixes skills and an orchestration workflow, and one of its skills carries a Snyk Fail; `imagekit-developer/skills` is official vendor tooling that pairs eight skills with three hosted MCP servers, and one indexed skill was removed from the repository in a later refactor, which the guide states rather than hides.
