# New Skills - October 31, 2026 Evening Sweep

Sweep of [skills.sh](https://skills.sh) for Hermes-relevant agent skills, cross-referenced against the existing catalog. The standing 20-query window returned 885 unique skills with 0 failed queries: 85 NEW, 267 PARTIAL.

The standing window was silent above the 1,000-install trigger for the twenty-sixth consecutive fire, its top NEW row reading 316 installs, and the morning fire still held 57 undocumented above-floor sources. This fire documented the sixteen largest of them, so no new probe was needed.

Every guide was built from a pre-verified pack holding the exact per-listing install figures, a depth-1 clone with its branch, licence and skill directories, and three-auditor verdicts sampled at five positions on skills.sh. Each draft was then checked with three gates: the format gate, the pack cross-check at a per-guide threshold, and the frontmatter audit. All 48 gate runs passed on the orchestrator re-run after an md5 comparison of the three canonical scripts.

## What The Batch Covers

The batch is weighted toward platform and language engineering rather than marketing tooling. Go, .NET, Neo4j, Metabase, Mojo, Zoho Catalyst, Kubernetes and Terraform account for the bulk of it, alongside three huge repositories whose skills ship as a by-product of the product: Google's Gemini CLI, Metabase and the 17,150-star Composio Codex index.

Five guides lead with a named auditor finding rather than filing it at the bottom: three Snyk Warns on sampled entries in the Composio index, a Trust Hub Warn on go-interfaces in the Go pack, Warns on all three auditors for ansible-validator, two Snyk Warns in the Gemini CLI sample and a Snyk Warn on Metabase's most installed listing. Index-versus-tree drift is carried factually in every affected guide, from the Composio index's 61 listings against 880 directories to Layers' nine bare-name listings with nothing to install.
