## Discovery Method

**Keyword sweep:** 45+ targeted `npx skills search` queries across hermes, agent, automation, social, marketing, email, MCP, and other Hermes-relevant categories.

- **Addy Osmani** (80K ⭐): Production-grade engineering skills from Google Chrome's engineering lead. The ci-cd-and-automation skill alone has 14.1K installs. Covers the full software development lifecycle from planning through shipping.
- **Intellectronica** (63K installs): Despite only 279 GitHub stars, the notion-api skill has 60K installs — making it one of the most-practical Notion integration skills available. The 22-skill toolkit covers knowledge management, developer tools, and creative utilities.
