**Source:** [indranilbanerjee/digital-marketing-pro](https://github.com/indranilbanerjee/digital-marketing-pro) (837⭐, pushed Sep 7, 2026)
**Skill:** `indranilbanerjee/digital-marketing-pro` (50 installable skills)
**Installs:** ~140 per top skill, ~4K combined (Sep 27, 2026 snapshot)
**Category:** Marketing / Growth Operations
**Quality Tier:** 🔵 Community (no skills.sh security verdicts published - verified Sep 27, 2026)

An open-source AI marketing operating system in skill form: 50 skills spanning strategy, SEO, AEO/GEO, paid media, content, CRM, and analytics. The suite covers the practitioner workflows - video scripts, paid advertising, landing-page audits, funnel architecture, competitor analysis, local SEO audits, reputation management, client proposals, onboarding, and PDF reporting. It is effectively a full-service digital agency's playbook, runnable by an agent.
