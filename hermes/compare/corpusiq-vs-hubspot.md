HubSpot runs your CRM. CorpusIQ is the layer that gives AI assistants live access to HubSpot data, alongside Shopify, QuickBooks, Stripe, GA4, and 40+ other business tools.

## When to use HubSpot alone

HubSpot is the right system for CRM, marketing, and sales pipeline management. It is built for that job and does it well. If that is your only need, HubSpot is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when pipeline is not the same thing as revenue. Deals sit in HubSpot. Payments sit in Stripe. Invoices sit in QuickBooks. Web traffic sits in GA4.

CorpusIQ connects HubSpot to those systems and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. ask "which HubSpot deals actually closed and got paid, and what did they cost to acquire" and get one source-cited answer.

## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace HubSpot?

No. HubSpot stays your system of record for its job. CorpusIQ reads from it with read-only OAuth. External-source retrieval tools are read-only; write-capable and control-plane tools are separately named and annotated.

### Can I use HubSpot data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect HubSpot once, then ask ChatGPT business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
