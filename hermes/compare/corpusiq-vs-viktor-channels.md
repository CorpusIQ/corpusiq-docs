# CorpusIQ vs Viktor Channels

Viktor is an AI employee that lives in Slack and Microsoft Teams. CorpusIQ is a data layer that works in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Slack, Teams, and anything that speaks MCP. One is an employee confined to the office chat. The other is a capability that follows you everywhere.

## Where Viktor works

Viktor installs into Slack or Microsoft Teams. That is the product: the AI employee appears in your workspace chat, connects your tools, and does work inside that app.

It works well inside those apps. The problem is everything outside them.

- No email: Viktor cannot email you a report, it only posts in Slack
- No browser: no web app for when you are not in Slack
- No mobile: the Slack app is the only surface
- No other AI assistants: Viktor does not appear in ChatGPT or Claude
- No other protocols: the answer lives in the workspace, not in the tools you use daily

## Where CorpusIQ works

CorpusIQ connects your business tools to the AI assistants you already use:

- ChatGPT: ask questions in ChatGPT and get cited answers from your data
- Claude: same connectors, same answers, same citations
- Perplexity: the same
- Slack and Teams: also works in your chat apps if that is where you live
- Any MCP client: the connectors speak standard MCP, so any MCP-compatible assistant works

One connection, every surface. The answer is the same everywhere because the data layer is the same everywhere.

## Why it matters

Businesses do not live in one app. The founder checks numbers in ChatGPT at night, the ops lead works in Teams during the day, the CFO asks Claude about cash flow on the road.

An AI employee locked inside Slack means the answer only exists where Slack exists. The moment someone asks a question in another tool, they are back to copy-pasting reports.

The consistent number is the whole point. If the same question returns the same cited answer in every assistant, the data layer is doing its job. If the answer only exists in one chat app, it is not an employee, it is a Slack bot.

## FAQ

### Is Slack enough for most teams?

Slack covers the office. It does not cover the airport, the CFO's phone, the ChatGPT tab every founder keeps open, or the client email that needs the number at 9 PM.

### Can I use CorpusIQ in Slack and ChatGPT at the same time?

Yes. Connect once, then ask in Slack, Teams, ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity. The data layer is shared; the surfaces are your choice.

### Does Viktor work outside Slack?

No. Viktor is Slack and Microsoft Teams only, by design.

### Which is better for a distributed team?

The one that is not locked to a single app. Distributed teams live in many tools, and the assistant needs to be wherever the question is asked.
