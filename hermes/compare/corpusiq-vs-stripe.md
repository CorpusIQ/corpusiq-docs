## When to use Stripe alone

Stripe is the right system for taking payments: charges, subscriptions, invoices, disputes, and payouts all live there. If your only need is billing, Stripe is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when revenue is not the same thing as cash. MRR sits in Stripe. Sales sit in Shopify. Costs sit in QuickBooks. Ad spend sits in Google Ads.

CorpusIQ connects Stripe to those systems and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Ask "how much of our Stripe revenue is actually profitable after Shopify fees and Google Ads spend" and get one source-cited answer.

### Does CorpusIQ replace Stripe?

No. Stripe stays your payment processor. CorpusIQ reads from it with read-only OAuth. Nothing is written back.

### Can I use Stripe data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect Stripe once, then ask ChatGPT business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my payment data safe?

Read-only access, scoped operational retention, zero stored customer files. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
