# CorpusIQ vs Viktor Pricing

Viktor sells an AI employee that lives in Slack and Microsoft Teams and charges by the credit. CorpusIQ sells a data layer for your existing AI assistants and charges a flat monthly rate. The pricing models are the difference between a predictable tool and a metered surprise.

## How Viktor prices

Viktor uses credit-based pricing. Every plan includes all features and all integrations; the only variable is how many credits you buy.

- Free tier: $100 in credits, no credit card
- Team plans: $50/month (20,000 credits) up to $5,000/month (2,000,000 credits); the most popular plan is $750/month for 300,000 credits
- Medium company: $7,500/month to $30,000/month
- Enterprise: $35,000/month to $50,000/month, custom terms beyond that

Credits are consumed per task. A quick task costs 100-300 credits. A complex workflow costs 500-1,500 credits. A full project can cost 2,000-5,000 credits.

The problem is not the credit concept. It is that your bill depends on how many tasks you run, and the only way to know what a task costs is to run it and watch the meter.

## How CorpusIQ prices

CorpusIQ is a flat monthly subscription. Same price whether you ask 10 questions a day or 500. The pricing is on the page, and the page does not change based on your usage.

- Solo: $29.95/month
- Team: $109.95/month
- Business: $189.95/month
- 30-day free trial, no credit card

## The real difference

| | Viktor | CorpusIQ |
|---|---|---|
| Billing model | Credits, consumed per task | Flat monthly |
| Price range | $50 to $50,000/month | $29.95 to $189.95/month |
| Cost per question | Unknown until you run it | Zero marginal cost |
| Plan ceiling | Usage caps every tier | Usage is included |
| Predictability | Meter-based | Fixed |

With Viktor, scaling usage means scaling your bill. With CorpusIQ, scaling usage means getting more value from the same plan.

## Why it matters

An AI assistant is only useful if you use it. A credit meter punishes exactly the behavior you want: asking more questions, checking more numbers, running more reports. The natural response to a meter is to ration your questions, which means the assistant stops being part of the workflow and becomes a treat you allow yourself.

Flat pricing removes the meter from the decision. You ask what you need, when you need it, because the cost is already paid.

## FAQ

### Is Viktor actually more expensive?

It depends on usage. For a small team running a handful of tasks a day, Viktor's $50 plan can be cheaper than CorpusIQ's Team plan. For any team that uses the tool regularly, the credit meter crosses CorpusIQ's flat price within weeks, and it keeps climbing from there.

### Do credits roll over?

Viktor free and trial credits never expire. Monthly plan credits reset each cycle, which means unused credits at the end of the month are gone. With CorpusIQ, there is nothing to roll over because there is nothing to meter.

### Which is better for a growing team?

Flat pricing. When a team grows, the number of questions grows with it. With CorpusIQ the bill stays the same while the value increases. With a meter, growth is a cost event.

### What is included in CorpusIQ plans?

All 40+ connectors, all AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity), source-cited answers, per-user scoping, and validation. Features are not gated by tier.
