# CorpusIQ vs ChatGPT Plugins: What Each One Does

ChatGPT plugins are back, and in 2026 they are the successor to custom GPTs rather than the one-app connectors they were in 2023. A plugin bundles reusable skills (instructions and workflow guidance) with connected apps, so a repeatable way of working travels with the tools it needs. Custom GPTs retire on December 11, 2026, and new GPT creation was cut off for Enterprise workspaces in October 2026.

That makes the comparison sharper than it used to be. A plugin is the packaging. CorpusIQ is the read layer the package reads from, and it is not confined to ChatGPT.

## Quick comparison

| Capability | Standalone | CorpusIQ |
|---|---|---|
| Primary job | reusable skills plus connected apps inside ChatGPT | Cross-source business answers in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity |
| Data access | Whichever apps the plugin connects, each authorized per account | Your live business tools via read-only OAuth |
| AI surface | ChatGPT Chat, Work, and Codex | Any AI you already use, via MCP |
| Answer format | Native output from its skills and apps | Source-cited plain-English answers |
| Data stored | Your records | Live retrieval, scoped retention, no raw customer files or full payloads |

## When to use it alone

A plugin is the right unit when you want one repeatable workflow, with its instructions and its apps, installable by a team inside a single ChatGPT workspace. If that is your only need, the plugin is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when the workflow needs numbers from more than one system. A plugin's connected apps each authorize one account: one app sees Shopify, another sees QuickBooks. Nobody sees both at once, and the numbers never reconcile.

CorpusIQ connects to those systems and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Ask a question that spans Shopify, QuickBooks, and GA4 in one prompt and get one cited answer. In ChatGPT it reaches a plugin or a custom connector as an app; in Claude and Perplexity it reaches the assistant the same way.

## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace it?

No. A plugin packages skills and apps inside ChatGPT; CorpusIQ is a read-only data layer those apps can use, and it also serves assistants outside ChatGPT. The two coexist.

### Can I use the data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect once, then ask business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
