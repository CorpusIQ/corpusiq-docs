Power BI builds business reports. CorpusIQ is the layer that gives AI assistants live access to your business data, alongside 40+ connected tools, in ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

## Quick comparison

| Capability | Power BI | CorpusIQ |
|---|---|---|
| Primary job | Business intelligence reporting and modeling | Cross-source business answers in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity |
| Data access | Its own data | Reads Power BI AND 40+ other tools in one prompt |
| AI surface | Power BI Copilot inside the report builder | Any AI you already use, via MCP |

## When to use Power BI alone

Power BI is the right system for Business intelligence reporting and modeling. It is built for that job and does it well. If that is your only need, Power BI is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when reports are not the same thing as answers. Power BI requires a data model, a report, and someone to maintain it. CorpusIQ answers the question directly, with citations.

CorpusIQ connects Power BI to those systems and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. ask "what is our true ROAS across Shopify, Google Ads, and GA4" and get a cited answer without building a single report.

## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace Power BI?

No. Power BI stays your system of record for its job. CorpusIQ reads from it with read-only OAuth. External-source retrieval tools are read-only; write-capable and control-plane tools are separately named and annotated.

### Can I use Power BI data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect Power BI once, then ask ChatGPT business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
