## When to use Northbeam alone

Northbeam is the right system for marketing attribution and ad spend analysis. It is built for that job and does it well. If that is your only need, Northbeam is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when attribution is one slice of the picture. Northbeam sees ad channels. CorpusIQ also reads QuickBooks, Stripe, and GA4 for the full financial view.

CorpusIQ connects Northbeam to those systems and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. ask "is our blended ROAS actually profitable after COGS" and get a cited answer across ads, revenue, and costs.

## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace Northbeam?

No. Northbeam stays your system of record for its job. CorpusIQ reads from it with read-only OAuth. External-source retrieval tools are read-only; write-capable and control-plane tools are separately named and annotated.

### Can I use Northbeam data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect Northbeam once, then ask ChatGPT business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
