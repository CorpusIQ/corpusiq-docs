## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace Microsoft Copilot?

No. Microsoft Copilot stays your system of record for its job. CorpusIQ reads from it with read-only OAuth. External-source retrieval tools are read-only; write-capable and control-plane tools are separately named and annotated.

### Can I use Microsoft Copilot data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect Microsoft Copilot once, then ask ChatGPT business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
