# CorpusIQ vs ChatGPT Enterprise: What Each One Does

ChatGPT Enterprise brings AI to work with security and admin features. CorpusIQ is the layer that gives ChatGPT live access to your business data with source-cited answers.

## Quick comparison

| Capability | Standalone | CorpusIQ |
|---|---|---|
| Primary job | enterprise AI assistant with compliance controls | Cross-source business answers in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity |
| Data access | Its own data | Your live business tools via read-only OAuth |
| AI surface | One assistant | Any AI you already use, via MCP |
| Answer format | Native reports | Source-cited plain-English answers |
| Data stored | Your records | Live retrieval, scoped retention, no raw customer files or full payloads |

## When to use it alone

It is the right system for its job. If that is your only need, it is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when the assistant cannot see your systems. ChatGPT Enterprise secures the AI. CorpusIQ connects it to your business data with read-only OAuth and citations.

CorpusIQ connects to those systems and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. ask ChatGPT a question across Shopify, GA4, and Google Ads and get one validated, cited answer.

## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace it?

No. It stays your system of record. CorpusIQ reads from it with read-only OAuth. External-source retrieval tools are read-only; write-capable and control-plane tools are separately named and annotated.

### Can I use the data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect once, then ask business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
