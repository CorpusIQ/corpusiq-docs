# CorpusIQ vs Glean: What Each One Does

Glean searches your company knowledge. CorpusIQ is the layer that gives AI assistants live access to your business data, alongside 40+ connected tools, in ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

## Quick comparison

| Capability | Glean | CorpusIQ |
|---|---|---|
| Primary job | enterprise knowledge search and AI assistant | Cross-source business answers in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity |
| Data access | Its own data | Reads Glean AND 40+ other tools in one prompt |
| AI surface | Glean Assistant inside the search product | Any AI you already use, via MCP |
| Answer format | Native reports | Source-cited plain-English answers |
| Data stored | Your records | Live retrieval, scoped retention, no raw customer files or full payloads |

## When to use Glean alone

Glean is the right system for enterprise knowledge search and AI assistant. It is built for that job and does it well. If that is your only need, Glean is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when documents are not the same thing as live numbers. Glean finds the file. CorpusIQ answers the question from live systems with citations.

CorpusIQ connects Glean to those systems and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. ask "what is our true ROAS across Shopify, Google Ads, and GA4" and get a cited answer from live connectors.

## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace Glean?

No. Glean stays your system of record for its job. CorpusIQ reads from it with read-only OAuth. External-source retrieval tools are read-only; write-capable and control-plane tools are separately named and annotated.

### Can I use Glean data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect Glean once, then ask ChatGPT business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
