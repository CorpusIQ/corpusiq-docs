## When to use Shopify alone

Shopify is the right system for running your store: catalog, checkout, orders, inventory, and Shopify Payments all live there. If your only need is selling, Shopify is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when you need the full picture. Revenue sits in Shopify. Ad spend sits in Google Ads and Meta. Costs sit in QuickBooks. Customer lifetime value sits across all of them.

CorpusIQ connects Shopify to those systems and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Ask "what is our true ROAS when Shopify revenue is netted against Google Ads spend and QuickBooks COGS" and get one source-cited answer.

## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace Shopify?

No. Shopify stays your storefront and order system. CorpusIQ reads from it with read-only OAuth. External-source retrieval tools are read-only; write-capable and control-plane tools are separately named and annotated.

### Can I use Shopify data in ChatGPT today?

Yes. Connect Shopify once, then ask ChatGPT business questions with live, cited answers.

### Is my store data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
