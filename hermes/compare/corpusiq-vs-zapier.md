# CorpusIQ vs Zapier: AI Business Data vs Workflow Automation

Both CorpusIQ and Zapier connect your business tools. But they solve fundamentally different problems. Understanding the difference helps you choose the right tool for the right job.

## The Core Difference

Zapier automates workflows between tools. When X happens in app A, do Y in app B. New Shopify order. Create QuickBooks invoice. Send Slack notification. Zapier is the plumbing between your apps.

CorpusIQ connects your tools to AI. When you ask ChatGPT a question about your business, CorpusIQ queries your actual tools and returns real data. It turns AI from a writing assistant into a business intelligence system.

Zapier moves data between tools. CorpusIQ lets AI read data from all your tools at once.

## When to Use Zapier

Zapier excels at triggered automation:
- New Stripe payment triggers a thank-you email
- New Typeform entry creates a HubSpot contact
- New Shopify order updates a Google Sheet
- Calendar event triggers a Slack reminder

If you need action A to trigger action B, Zapier is the right tool.

## When to Use CorpusIQ

CorpusIQ excels at AI-powered business intelligence:
- Ask ChatGPT: "What was revenue last month across all channels?"
- Ask Claude: "Which customers are at risk of churning?"
- Ask Perplexity: "What is our actual blended ROAS?"

If you need AI to answer questions about your business using live data from your tools, CorpusIQ is the right tool.

## Can You Use Both?

Yes. They are complementary, not competitive. Zapier handles the workflows. CorpusIQ handles the intelligence.

A business could use Zapier to automate order processing and CorpusIQ to answer strategic questions about revenue, customers, and marketing performance. Different tools. Different problems. Both valuable.

## Key Differences

| | CorpusIQ | Zapier |
|---|---|---|
| Primary function | AI business intelligence | Workflow automation |
| How it works | AI queries your tools live | Trigger-action sequences |
| Data direction | Read-only from tools to AI | Write between tools |
| AI integration | ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity | Limited AI features |
| Metric consistency | Canonical definitions across tools | Per-workflow logic |
| Source citations | Every answer traced to origin | Not applicable |
| Pricing | From $29.95/month | From $19.99/month |
| Free trial | 30 days, no credit card | 14 days |

## The Bottom Line

If you spend Monday mornings pulling reports from four different tools and reconciling them in a spreadsheet, you need CorpusIQ. If you have repetitive workflows that follow the same pattern every time, you need Zapier. Most growing businesses need both.
