Make.com automates workflows visually. CorpusIQ is the layer that gives AI assistants live access to your business data, alongside 40+ connected tools, in ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

## Quick comparison

## When to use Make.com alone

Make.com is the right system for visual workflow automation and app connections. It is built for that job and does it well. If that is your only need, Make.com is the answer.

## When to add CorpusIQ

The pain starts when automation is not the same thing as answers. Make moves data between apps. CorpusIQ explains what the data means across all of them at once.

CorpusIQ reads those systems live and exposes all of them to ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. Ask "why did revenue drop last week" and get a cited answer drawn from Shopify, GA4, and QuickBooks in one prompt.

## FAQ

### Does CorpusIQ replace Make.com?

No. Make.com stays your system of record for its job. CorpusIQ reads your business systems directly with read-only OAuth. External-source retrieval tools are read-only; write-capable and control-plane tools are separately named and annotated.

### Can I use Make.com data in ChatGPT today?

There is no Make.com connector in CorpusIQ. What does connect: 40+ business tools read-only, each answer carrying the source record behind the number.

### Is my data safe?

Read-only OAuth. No retained raw customer files or full connector payloads; operational logs and indexes are disclosed separately. CASA Tier 2 certified by DEKRA, hosted on Microsoft Azure.
