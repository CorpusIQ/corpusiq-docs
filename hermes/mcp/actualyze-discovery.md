# Actualyze — AI Governance Layer Joins MCP Ecosystem

**Discovered:** August 3, 2026

Actualyze is a new AI governance platform that sits between your applications and your inference calls. It governs, secures, operates, and optimizes every AI request.

## What It Does

- **Govern**: Access control, approvals, budgets per team or project
- **Secure**: Guardrails, PII masking, audit logs, compliance
- **Operate**: Model catalog, deployment management, full observability
- **Optimize**: Intelligent model routing, failover, cost tracking

One endpoint swap. Zero code changes. Works with OpenAI SDK and all major model providers.

## Why It Matters for MCP

As MCP adoption grows, enterprises need governance over their AI tool usage. Actualyze provides the operations layer — which models are called, how much they cost, whether they leak data.

Combined with MCP servers like CorpusIQ (business data), the result is governed, secure AI with live business answers.
