| [FindingSteel MCP](/hermes/mcp/servers/external/findingsteel-mcp/) | Manufacturing & Industrial | The Chinese GB standard steel section reference and calculator: search 143 GB/T section records by category or keyword, look up one exact designation for its section properties (A, Ix, Iy, Wx, Wy, ix, iy and kg/m), compute a welded I-section or box section from plate dimensions, calculate theoretical weight for plate, round, square, flat, hexagon, octagon, rebar and pipe shapes, and read the built-in material density table. Free and keyless over Streamable HTTP at `www.findingsteel.com/mcp/` |
