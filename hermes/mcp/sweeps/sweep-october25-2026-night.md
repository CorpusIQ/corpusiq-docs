# MCP Server Discovery - October 25, 2026 (Night Sweep)

The counter moved. After a full day of reading 17,243 servers at every capture, mcpservers.org served 17,354 at the 04:01Z read on October 25, an addition of 111 inside roughly twelve hours. A directory that grows by a hundred in a night is a directory whose first pages have been rewritten, so this pass read wide and shallow: `/all` pages one to six, and then judged what it found against the catalog, every sweep report and the last-sweep ledger before probing anything.

Three entries clear the bar. Twelve of the seventy names that appeared in no prior record were worth a live probe; three survived it.

The three are the same finding from three angles: a work item with a deadline attached, a draft with a score attached, a valuation with a hash attached. Each is a case of the record carrying its own evidence rather than a claim about the record.

## Source Behaviour Worth Recording
