    MCP Server Discovery - November 25, 2026 (Evening)¶

    Fired on the 21:00 MST tick, seventeen hours after the midday capture read the same surface and eight hours after the last build finished deploying. The directory did not grow in this window. It restamped 736 distinct listings, lost one, and the restamps are arithmetic rather than impression: every stamped key was already in the overnight baseline. The three ships came from the two-day cohort that the re-shard created, resolved through the official registry and driven end to end before a word was written.

    The raw count fell by 111 and not one server arrived. That is not a contradiction, it is the shard arithmetic again: servers/13 slipped from 220 keys to 219 and the other twelve maps rebalanced their raw <loc> counts while holding their distinct key counts identical (1,987 / 1,990 / 1,994 / 1,999 / 1,996 / 2,000 x7). Distinct keys per map are the stable measure; raw <loc> entries are route variants, locale prefixes and duplicate submissions of the same slug, and they move whenever a map is rewritten.

    github-mcp-server is not a server that appeared this evening. It is one of the best-known entries in the ecosystem, it sits in the priority map, and the two baselines before this one both contain it:

    The overnight pass wrote a baseline that lost the 34 priority-map keys, and every later diff inherited a 34-key phantom arrival. Today's capture writes the union of the thirteen maps and the priority map, so the next pass starts clean. The lesson is the one this log keeps relearning in a new costume: when a diff reports a suspiciously round cohort, check whether the cohort is a map.

    The three ships¶

    All three are keyless, all three were driven on this pass, and each one carries a defect that the guide records rather than smooths.

    Suppliers Net MCP - Wholesale Prices With the Seller and the Date has no gate at all: initialize 200 and a 4-tool tools/list at https://suppliers.net/mcp (registry io.github.Dragoonhawx/suppliers-net 1.0.0). The catalogue states itself as 24,681 current advertised prices from 427 sellers across 804 suppliers in eight states summing exactly (266 + 222 + 156 + 74 + 56 + 15 + 10 + 5). Twelve priced rows were driven on a beef query and the unit arithmetic reproduces on all twelve ($7.09 / 5 kg = $1.418/kg, $11.50 / 3 kg = $3.8333/kg, $5.60 / 1.05 kg = $5.3333/kg), ordered cheapest per kilo first across mixed pack sizes with the seller, the source URL and the date_seen on every row. Three refusals were measured and each names its own limit: market_rates returns count: 0 until three verified suppliers list the same product, a nonsense query returns "Suppliers Net does not guess a price", and a bogus category fails closed at total: 0. One defect recorded: category is matched against product text rather than the seller's category field, so category: "Meat" in NSW returns Claudio's Seafoods on the strength of MUSSEL MEAT and Prawn Meat, and category: "Seafood" returns a Pantry supplier, out of a 266-supplier state.

    Qonvert MCP - Tracking Parameters Checked After Consent is the discovery-open tier split cleanly in two. An 18-tool tools/list arrives without a key (registry ai.qonvert/qonvert 1.0.1), and three of the eighteen run without an account: the tracking check, the connect guide and the free placement guidance. check_marketing_tracking_url names exactly which of the three core UTM parameters are missing from a supplied URL and reports click identifiers in a separate field, with the note that "a missing identifier in this sample is not evidence of a tracking gap" because the ad platform adds it at click time. The other fifteen need an OAuth grant and refuse in band, get_project returning 401 with "Create a Qonvert account or sign in to connect your project and its sources." Three defects recorded: tools/list answers 400 to a client pinning protocol 2025-06-18 and 200 to 2025-11-25, which reads as a broken server rather than a version mismatch; get_marketing_setup_guide returns "Invalid tool or arguments." on five of five attempts including inputs built from its own schema, so a listed tool cannot succeed; and the free-guidance enums are market: [ru, global] and goal: [visibility, feedback, discovery], so a US campaign falls outside the tool's scope and gets an empty list.

    Prismintel MCP - Four-Bloc Triage of Cached Geopolitical Dossiers is a two-tool keyless read of a cache (registry app.prismintel/triage 1.0.0) whose own instructions say "Read-only, zero-cost access to cached PRISMINTEL multi-bloc dossiers." One 51-source dossier at a 68% consensus index returned a three-part BLUF plus a bloc split of 39 / 29 / 20 / 12% with tone scores -15 / -10 / -55 / +5 and the omission each bloc makes, summing to 100. The finding is freshness and it is arithmetic: all nine theaters carried is_expired: true and freshness: "archive" at read time, the newest compiled 11.5 hours earlier and the oldest 56.5 hours earlier, while the same payload reported status: CACHED_DOSSIER_AVAILABLE - an agent branching on status presents a 30-hour-old analysis as current. Two further defects: the Hormuz dossier occupies two rows under one title (68% and 62%, seventeen minutes apart), and corpusSnapshotHash is 16 hex characters where a SHA-256 has 64. A restraint paragraph is the best thing in the payload: the dossier states what its evidence does not establish, in the same object as the claim.

    The push surfaces¶

    mcp.so answered /feed 200 at 160,800 bytes with 43 /servers/ links collapsing to 36 distinct slugs and 30 distinct stamps. Four cards sit above the previous top: print-pronto-data, teledirectmd-0266ca (10:01 PM), atono-d236de (09:08 PM) and zooop (08:54 PM), then urml at 11:00 AM as the oldest of the new group. The homepage carried no slug or createdAt payload for the same reason.

    • mcp.so: /servers/corpusiq-docs → 200, 857,169 bytes, 175 occurrences of corpusiq. The singular guesses stay 404 (/servers/corpusiq → 404, 23,986 bytes), which is the recorded shape and not a delisting.

    What the pass says about the platform¶

    Two movements and no arrivals is the honest reading. The directory restamped 736 distinct listings in seventeen hours and added none, one listing was retired, and the only cohort that produced anything drivable was the two-day population from the re-shard. A stamp is a touch, not a birth: 1,098 rows moved onto 736 keys that were all present in the baseline overnight, and the previous pass made the same measurement with a different cohort. The pattern to keep is the one that caught the 34 phantoms - when a diff reports a round cohort, ask which map it came from - because the alternative is a catalog that congratulates itself every evening on servers it documented in September.
