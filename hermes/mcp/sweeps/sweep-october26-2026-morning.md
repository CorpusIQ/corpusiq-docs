# MCP Server Discovery - October 26, 2026 (Morning Sweep)

The listing that had been frozen through the whole of Sunday came back with a moderation batch. mcpservers.org served 17,522 servers at the 10:0xZ captures against 17,360 at the 23:26Z read nine hours earlier, and the counter did not hold still inside a single pass: page three answered 17,521 and page four answered 17,354, which is a batch being applied while it was being read rather than three different directories. Eight pages were read, 240 cards, and every card that resolved to a name already in this catalog matched on the name rather than the slug, because the registry derives its URLs from domains and this catalog names products.

Three entries earned a guide, and each needed a different proof: one answered without a key and was driven four times against our own domain, one published its capability in discovery documents because its tools sit behind a paywall, and one published a discovery document that sits a layer away from the URL its own authentication header points at.
