## The window, measured

The map counts moved and they moved in one place. Every one of the twelve server maps carried exactly its overnight count except three: **`servers/11` from 601 to 954 (+353)**, `servers/10` from 2,604 to 2,606 (+2) and `servers/8` from 6,588 to 6,589 (+1). The newest entries append to the highest-numbered map, which is why a wave shows up there and why a per-map count is a faster arrival signal than a total.

The diff against the **21:00 MST capture kept on disk** gave **294 additions and 3 removals**, and the removals are the normalisation pair this catalog has recorded before: three slugs the baseline carried with a `%23` fragment (`ahmedkhaleel2004/gitdiagram`, `orcasecurity/orca-skills`, `podop29/shotpipe-mcp`) now arrive in their `#` form. Three keys changed spelling, no product left.

## Catalogued: 3 servers, 3 guides

- [Bullion Price Check MCP - Dealer Metal Pricing and Stock](/hermes/mcp/servers/external/bullion-price-check-mcp/) - remote Streamable HTTP at `https://bullionpricecheck.com/mcp`, registry record `com.bullionpricecheck/bullion-price-check` with **2.0.0** as the newest published version while the endpoint answers as **2.1.0**, which is the reverse of the usual drift. Keyless end to end: `initialize` **200**, `tools/list` **200** with **27** tools all annotated `readOnlyHint: true`, and `tools/call` **200** with no credential. Six calls were driven. `get_spot_prices` returned gold **4,002.80**, silver **52.38** and platinum **1,551** USD per troy ounce with a gold/silver ratio of **76.42**, a stated upstream source, a per-metal timestamp and a freshness block reading `spot_age_minutes: 70` against a 120-minute threshold, plus an explicit note that **palladium is not tracked** so an assistant cannot substitute a guess. `get_dealer_prices` on the 1 oz Silver Buffalo Round returned six ranked dealers against a melt of **52.38**: **4.57 percent** over melt at the low (MintBuilder, 54.77) against **20.59 percent** at the top (APMEX, 63.16), a spread of **8.39 USD** on a 52.38 USD item, with three of the six dealers carrying a `Partner` label and ranking 2, 4 and 5, which is the live proof of the instruction string's claim that a partner **pays to list, never to rank**. Two dealers were excluded and both reasons were operational: Hero Bullion `out_of_stock`, and Kitco at **471 minutes old** against a 60-minute limit for that source. The Premium Index is the operator insight: on the same five silver products the **median dealer asks two to two and a half times the lowest premium**, and the one-ounce round is the worst case at **4.57 percent low against a 10.09 percent median**. `true_cost` priced ten units at 3,200 with a 45 shipping charge and a 3.5 percent card fee as **33,165 USD delivered, 3,316.50 per unit**, where the card surcharge alone is **1,120**. `bullion_sales_tax` answered Arizona as **exempt** with the rule text and a 51-jurisdiction table whose statuses are not binary (**partial** in California, Connecticut and Florida; **taxable** in DC and Hawaii). Two defects recorded rather than smoothed: `fair_offer_check` rejects a `product` string that its own schema advertises (`EXPECTED_OBJECT`) while refusing to invent a number, and shipping and payment fees are absent from the dealer ladder. First bullion entry here.
- [TouristPads MCP - Singapore Event Rental Quotes](/hermes/mcp/servers/external/touristpads-mcp/) - remote Streamable HTTP at `https://touristpads.com/mcp`, registry record `com.touristpads/touristpads` **1.0.0**, keyless, and the smallest useful surface of the pass: **three** tools, `list_rental_options` and `price_rental` annotated read-only and `request_quote` the only write. `list_rental_options` returned **16** product ids with maximum quantities (**104** iPad 10.2 bodies, **55** with table stands, **30** with floor stands, **22** 5G routers, **3** iPhone 17s), four accessories, three charged extras and **ten** rules. A six-iPad, two-router, three-day order was priced end to end: equipment **1,650.72 SGD**, delivery **106.80**, after-hours **150**, Sunday leg **500**, rounding **-0.52**, total **2,407.00 SGD**, where the equipment is **68.6 percent** of the bill and the calendar is **31.4 percent**. The server then volunteered a `cheaperTimingOptions` entry that moves the Sunday collection to Monday 10:30 for **1,907.00 SGD**, exactly **500** less, and the initialize instruction string tells the assistant to surface it. Three readings make this an operator entry rather than a price page: the estimate states **"no quote has been issued"**, `afterHoursChargeNeedsAgreement` is set because a customer must agree to an after-hours charge before a quote exists, and the rules publish the levers that decide the invoice - a **280 SGD** equipment minimum, **75 SGD per 30 minutes** outside 9am to 6pm per leg, **500 SGD** for a Sunday or public-holiday leg, **8 percent off at 2,000** and **10 percent at 3,000** of equipment applied automatically, and location surcharges (Sentosa, Tuas, Jurong Island 25 per trip, Changi Exhibition Centre 60) **added at signing and not in the quote**. The order above stopped at 1,650.72 and earned no volume discount, which is the other finding. First event-equipment rental entry here.
