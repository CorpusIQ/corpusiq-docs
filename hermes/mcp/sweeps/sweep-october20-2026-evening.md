# MCP Server Discovery - October 20, 2026 (Evening Sweep)

Evening delta about seven hours after the midday capture and about six after the supplement that followed it, which closed the chatmcp/mcpso issue window and catalogued MuniSpot and XposedOrNot. The mcp.so `/feed` page 1 returned 30 cards, ten of them posted inside the window and the newest at 17 minutes. mcpservers.org `/all` pages 1 to 3 served about 90 cards against a stated total of 16,484, and the spoilers at the top of that list are still the same sponsor slots, so the position of the catalogued `drift` card remained the useful marker for what had been added since the morning.

The sweep produced one story rather than four. A single vendor published seven servers within the same hour, all aimed at the same customer, and together they close a gap this catalog has carried since it started tracking operations software: a business with many locations, whose records are legal obligations rather than transactions.

## New Servers Catalogued

| Server | Category | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [Copliancy MCP Suite](/hermes/mcp/servers/external/copliancy-mcp/) | Operations | Seven remote servers from Copliancy Inc, one per product, catalogued on one page because they share a customer, a data model built around locations, and an approval convention for writes. **IncidentVault** (`https://app.incidentvault.com/api/mcp/apps/server`) holds incidents, claims, reserves, settlements, legal spend, risk checks, regulatory inspections and tasks, with 19 read and 19 write scopes plus `offline_access`. **PolicyTrak** (`https://app.policytrak.com/api/mcp`) holds sites, compliance fields, policies, SOPs, laws, acknowledgements, workflows, files and audit trails across 57 scopes. **RetailHardHat** (`https://app.retailhardhat.com/api/mcp`) runs a new site from due diligence through permits, bids, RFIs, project budgets, change requests and invoices to opening, in 20 scopes. **CredentialCamp** (`https://app.credentialcamp.com/api/mcp`) tracks employee credentials, certifications and mandated training in 5. **QuipAssist** (`https://app.quipassist.com/api/mcp`) is the maintenance layer: equipment, work orders, preventive schedules, parts, vendors and IoT readings in 13, one of them `documents:sensitive`. **LeaseCommand** (`https://app.leasecommand.com/api/mcp`) carries leases, rent schedules, critical dates and ASC 842 amortization with journal entries. **SmartShifts** (`https://app.smartshifts.com/api/mcp/apps/server`) covers shifts, coverage, time off and tasks. Verified live this sweep: all seven endpoints answered a `POST initialize` probe with 401 or 403 and never a 200 without credentials. Five publish OAuth protected-resource metadata; SmartShifts returns 404 on that path and LeaseCommand serves its application HTML there. IncidentVault's metadata document names 38 scopes, `bearer_methods_supported: ["header"]` and `resource_documentation` at its provider docs, and its authorization server publishes authorize, token, registration and revocation endpoints for PKCE S256 with dynamic client registration. Its provider docs state the distinction that makes the suite safe to hand to a store manager: the operational endpoint returns opaque record IDs, allowlisted status and priority, dates, location IDs and audit percentages, excluding names, narratives, medical details, custom fields and attachments, while the private endpoint keeps the full authorized catalog. Writes are opt-in and every modifying tool produces a saved preview a person approves inside the product; the docs are explicit that pending or approved is not executed success. Free sign-up, tier pricing not published in text. **Catalog gap closed:** A4B, ZenSched and STEADYWRK cover assets, workforce scheduling and field dispatch as single-purpose tools, and nothing in this catalog carried license and permit renewals, incident and claims records, ASC 842 lease accounting, or credential compliance across locations. |
