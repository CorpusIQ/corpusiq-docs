Three access shapes per tool: read, change (acts at once, needs an idempotency key) and proposal for approval. A proposal returns status: awaiting_approval, an actionId, a field-by-field preview, an expiresAt and an approval link at adrails.ai/oauth/actions/<actionId>; any current owner or admin can approve it signed in as themselves, Adrails re-reads the platform and applies the exact change once or blocks it if the entity moved, and the proposal expires 24 hours after it was prepared (30 minutes in-app). Budget changes are capped at 30% each, an increase needs measured spend and results, bulk selection covers at most 40 entities, and everything created starts paused. Idempotency is enforced rather than advisory, errors come back as tool results with isError so the model can correct the call, a result over 256,000 bytes is refused whole with no partial figures, and limits run 60 calls a minute per token, 10 changes a minute, 180 a minute per person and 600 per workspace. Business sources read beside ad spend: Shopify, Stripe, Klaviyo, Triple Whale, PostHog, RevenueCat and Google Drive. Two tools carry the entry. store_revenue returns gross, refunded and net sales kept apart in each store's own currency and never converted. business_review accepts an explanation only when every number quotes its source word for word, from the person's own statement or from a tool result in the connection's history, which is a provenance rule enforced inside the tool rather than a disclaimer beside it. budget_recommend compares the latest two complete seven-day windows in the account's time zone and suggests no amount. Pricing from the vendor's own page: Preview free with 10 credits once, Starter $49 a month with 200 credits, Growth $149 with 700, Scale $399 with 2,000, unlimited ad accounts and team members on every paid plan, every connector included, no percentage of ad spend, and MCP tool calls cost zero credits. Gap named rather than claimed: no tool call was made. Every tool needs an OAuth connection to a workspace with a connected ad account, so the entry rests on the live 401, the protected-resource metadata, the server card and the vendor's 69-tool reference, and the absent execution is stated in the entry.
