# MCP Server Discovery - October 21, 2026 (Night Sweep)

Night delta about six hours after the evening capture. The three discovery surfaces disagreed for the first time this week, which is the useful part of the result: the mcp.so `/feed` page 1 returned 30 cards with six posted inside the window, the newest 29 minutes old at capture, while mcpservers.org `/all` pages 1 to 4 returned 120 cards with nothing business-relevant that the feed had not already carried, and the chatmcp/mcpso issue window that the previous two passes each recorded as not run had 43 submissions behind it, #5817 through #5861.

Page 4 of `/all` was run for the first time. It carried no new business class, only consumer, regional and developer utilities, which is why two passes in a row could skip it without losing an entry. That is now recorded rather than assumed.

**The rule that decided the four.** A server earns a page when the operator would otherwise have to build the join by hand: the lease that says what should have been paid, the page that is the product, the address that must resolve to a real tract, or the funnel that must be instrumented after it is measured. Frequency of listing is not the signal. Three of the four were settled by calling the endpoint and watching what came back, and the one that was not (Privatrak, behind a credential) is marked accordingly rather than described as verified.
