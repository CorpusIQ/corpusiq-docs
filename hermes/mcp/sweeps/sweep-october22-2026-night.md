# MCP Server Discovery - October 22, 2026 (Night Sweep)

Night delta about six hours after the evening capture. The two surfaces disagreed in a way worth recording: mcpservers.org `/all` pages 1 to 3 came back with 85 unique slugs and every one of them matched a disposition already in the ledger, while the mcp.so `/feed` page 1 carried eight cards from the previous record's window that no sweep page had ever named. The feed, not the directory, was the only surface producing work this cycle.

The second finding is a correction to how a card is judged. All three catalogued entries were settled by running a real tool call rather than a handshake, and in one case the real call contradicted the card that advertised it.
