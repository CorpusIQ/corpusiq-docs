### Form 5500 MCP - US Retirement Plan Filings in Chat

Every retirement and welfare plan filed with the U.S. Department of Labor is public, but the questions operators ask are comparative - what plans of my size pay in service-provider fees, how a plan's financials moved year over year, whether a sponsor looks worth approaching. This server answers those questions with filed figures only. Nine tools: search plans by sponsor name, plan name, EIN, state, plan type or subtype, form type, participant band or asset band; read one plan's full filed profile; rank plans by a filed metric; walk a plan's field-level filing history; compare a cohort by percentile with a box plot that marks one plan; and run eight guided skills - Form 5500 literacy, plan fee benchmark, plan diligence brief, fee transparency report, compliance flag scan, committee review inputs, verify a plan claim and plan prospecting screen. For HR and benefits teams, advisors and recordkeepers, M&A diligence and plan committees.

Relevance: HIGH. **Endpoint live** (POST initialize to `https://5500mcp.ai/mcp` returns 200 keyless as `5500mcp` v1.0.0; `tools/list` returns all nine tools), **keyless** (no account, no key; public DOL filings), **vendor docs live** (5500mcp.ai 200) and **updated daily** (dataset vintage 2026-09-03). The server reports exactly as filed and its instructions forbid estimating, modelling, projecting or imputing any figure a plan did not file. An adjacent wrapper of the same EFAST2 search service is published as `io.github.pipeworx-io/form5500`.

### AIData.Broker MCP - AI Training Data Prices and Deals

A cited price book for the AI data market. Nine read-only tools answer what a kind of training data actually sells for, who is buying, which licences were disclosed, what the courts have said and what a business system's own terms allow: market rates (median, middle half and range) by kind of data and side of market; the individual published price observations behind those rates; a low-to-high dollar range for a comparable holding with the arithmetic shown; buyers and their published terms; vendors of training data, RL environments, evaluations and annotation; the licences disclosed to AI companies since July 2023; lawsuits, rulings, settlements and regulatory actions over AI training data; and the AI-training terms of named business systems (Slack, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Zoom, GitHub and others). Datasets are open under CC BY 4.0. For data holders, legal and procurement, policy and compliance, and finance or strategy work on an asset class that rarely appears in a model.

Relevance: MEDIUM-HIGH. **Endpoint live** (POST initialize to `https://aidata.broker/mcp` returns 200 keyless as `AIData.Broker` v2026.10.14; `tools/list` returns all nine tools, every one flagged read-only), **keyless and read-only** (no account, no key; nothing writable), **vendor docs and the server page live** (aidata.broker and aidata.broker/mcp-server both 200) and **evidence-bound by construction** (each answer names its source and the page to cite, and no median is returned where the published evidence does not support one).
