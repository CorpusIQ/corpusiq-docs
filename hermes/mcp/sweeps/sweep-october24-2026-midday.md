# MCP Server Discovery - October 24, 2026 (Midday Sweep)

Six hours after the night capture, and the directory was flat. mcpservers.org still counted 17,243 servers, so `/all` pages 1 to 3 returned 86 unique slugs and every one of them carried a prior disposition. The feed served four cards past the night capture, two of which the night pass had already read. The submission window advanced fourteen issues.

One entry cleared the bar, and it is the first surface in this catalog where a music catalog, its pitches, its licenses and its royalty statements sit behind one server.

## New Servers Catalogued

| Server | Category | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [DropCue MCP](/hermes/mcp/servers/external/dropcue-mcp/) | Media | Music catalog and rights operations for composers, producers, publishers and sync agencies, over one local stdio server that talks to `https://dropcue.app/api/v1`. The kit is published publicly at `https://dropcue.app/kit/dropcue-kit.tar.gz` and was downloaded, installed and driven in this sweep: `initialize` returned `serverInfo` `dropcue` 0.19.1 on protocol 2025-06-18 with the tools capability and `listChanged`, and `tools/list` returned 105 tools, the same count the kit's own `version.json` declares, with the API routes behind each documented in its `openapi.yaml` (4,057 lines). The business record set: `search_tracks` with bulk `update_track_metadata` and `nest_versions`, `preview_upload` and `replace_audio` that show a plan first and keep the previous master for 30 days, the pitch pipeline through `list_pitches`, `log_follow_up` and `create_pitch`, tracked share links where `add_share_recipient` gives each supervisor a `?r=` link and their plays show under their name, the License Tracker through `list_licenses`, `add_license`, `license_statuses`, `attach_license_file` and agreements with delivery checklists, the Royalty Tracker through `import_royalty_statement` (a PRO or publisher CSV up to 4 MB and 50,000 lines, dry run first, unknown layouts returned with their column headers so the user names the title and amount columns) and `royalty_summary` (totals by PRO, network, show, usage, society and territory, with top and bottom earning tracks), `report_summary` setting opens, plays, listeners and downloads against the same number of days before, and `whats_next` deriving overdue, soon, waiting and no-date attention items from the records themselves while stating that plays and downloads never appear there. Safety enforced in the tool surface rather than promised in prose: nothing among the 105 deletes a track, playlist, contact or company (removal archives), there is no send tool at all, writers, publishers and ISRCs are never changed with `fill_track_credits` filling empty fields only, the only delete is an unpublished portfolio draft, and the two public actions, a share link and a portfolio publish, stop for the user's confirmation. Auth is a personal API key read from `DROPCUE_API_TOKEN` or the macOS Keychain item `dropcue-api-token`, never written into a result, an error or a log line. Priced as paid DropCue Studio from $29 a month billed annually with the API and the assistant connection included and trials excluded, video intake on Pro or higher, `make_stems` at one stem credit a run and unavailable on Starter and Artist. **Gap named rather than claimed:** no tool that reaches the API was called, because every one needs a paid account with a personal key this sweep does not create; the entry rests on the published kit, its real `tools/list`, its OpenAPI spec, the vendor's docs and pricing, and the vendor's dated September 28, 2026 comparison that puts DISCO's API on Enterprise only and says ReelCrafter lists no API. No official registry entry exists: a `registry.modelcontextprotocol.io` search for dropcue returned zero servers on October 24, 2026. |

## Source Behaviour Worth Recording

The directory count did not move between the night capture at `10:01Z` and this pass at `13:22Z`: 17,243 servers both times, while the `/all` pages served 86 unique slugs against the night pass's 87. A flat count with a shifting page is the ordinary churn of one card entering as another leaves, and it is the reason a same-day repeat sweep treats `/all` as a cross-check rather than a discovery lane. The productive lane at this gap was the submission window, which carried every one of the fourteen candidates.

The second thing worth recording is a source that resolves a candidate without an account. DropCue publishes its entire MCP kit as a tarball, so a local server that has no hosted endpoint to probe can still be read at `tools/list` and counted against the vendor's own declared total. That is a stronger evidence tier than a documentation read and a fairer one than a hold on the grounds that nothing could be probed.
