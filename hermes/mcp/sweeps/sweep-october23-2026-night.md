## Source Behaviour Worth Recording

Two things about the surfaces are worth more than the count.

**The feed's overnight rate is two cards in seven hours**, against six in the ten hours the evening capture covered. Neither card was a repeat of an unseen earlier one, which reverses the failure the October 22 night sweep recorded: at that gap, eight cards had been fetched by an earlier pass and never dispositioned. Reading the feed twice at a short gap and diffing entry by entry is still the only reliable way to catch that, and this cycle's diff was clean.

**A directory card is a claim and the endpoint is the test.** mcp.so carried Nexscope as verified and featured with Authentication: None, and the endpoint requires a bearer token. The same cycle produced the mirror case: Nexscope's own `www-authenticate` header points at a protected-resource document that does not exist, while the authorization-server document one path over is complete. A client that trusts the card fails to authenticate; a client that trusts the header fails to discover. Both failures are silent in the tooling, so the only defence is the probe and the two metadata fetches, and the catalog entry has to state the authentication rather than inherit it.
