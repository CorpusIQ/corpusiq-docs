# MCP Server Discovery - October 25, 2026 (Late Sweep)

Five hours and twenty-six minutes separate this pass from the midday supplement, and the directory did not move at all: mcpservers.org served 17,360 servers at 18:00Z and the same 17,360 at the 23:26Z capture, with page one carrying the identical thirty cards in the identical order. When the listing is frozen the breadth work buys nothing, so this pass spent its reads where the day actually moved: the mcp.so feed, which rotated five new cards into page one after the supplement's cutoff, and the chatmcp/mcpso submission window, which filed twenty-one issues between `#6111` and `#6131`.

Three of them answer to a business operator rather than to a builder, and all three are different kinds of evidence. One was driven end to end without a key. Two are OAuth gated, so their capability is read from the discovery documents the servers publish about themselves.

## Directory Behaviour

The counter did not move in five and a half hours: 17,360 at the midday capture, 17,360 at 23:26Z, and page one byte-for-byte the same thirty cards. That is the second frozen window today after the fifty minute gap at midday, which makes the mechanism clear enough to state: mcpservers.org publishes in moderation batches, so on a day when the batch lands early the listing is a fixed surface for the rest of it, and the submission window is the only living feed. Page two and page three were read again and every card on them resolves to a prior disposition from today's midday pass, which read all ninety.
