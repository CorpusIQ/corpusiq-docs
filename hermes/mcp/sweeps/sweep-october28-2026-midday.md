# MCP Server Discovery - October 28, 2026 (Midday Sweep)

Seven hours after the morning pass, the mcpservers.org counter read **17,913 again**, unchanged for the second consecutive window and the third consecutive read going back to 02:04 GMT. The composition underneath it moved hard: thirty slugs left pages one to three and thirty new ones arrived, which is a full page turnover with no net addition to the index. That combination is worth naming because it inverts the usual read of that surface. A stable counter with a churning page means the directory is re-ranking and re-slugging what it already holds, not ingesting, and the thirty new slugs resolved to guide pages, card endpoints, documentation routes and regional oddities rather than to new servers. Breadth cost three fetches and returned no catalogue entry.

The delta came from the two surfaces that did move. The feed's newest card was stamped `2026-10-28T15:39:45Z`, five and a half hours past the morning's newest, and the submission window advanced from `#6286` to `#6322`, thirty-six issues in seven hours. Of those, three were documentation maintenance rather than submissions (`#6289`, `#6290`, `#6292`), which leaves thirty-three substantive claims to read.

## What Was Catalogued

Six entries earned guides. Every endpoint in this batch was probed rather than trusted, and one probe contradicted its own directory card.

[HighLevel Agency MCP](/hermes/mcp/servers/external/highlevel-agency-mcp/) is the entry this cycle exists for, and it is a correction. The mcpservers.org card advertises **481 tools across 39 domain families**. `https://ghlagencymcp.com/mcp` answered a bare `initialize` with **HTTP 200** keyless, `serverInfo` name `highlevel-agency-mcp`, version `1.0.0`, on protocol `2025-06-18`, and `tools/list` returned **thirteen tools**. The thirteen are not a subset of an API proxy, they are a differently shaped product: `ghl_snapshot_diff_audit` compares a live client sub-account against a reference Master Snapshot **without overwriting assets**, `ghl_custom_values_audit` reads Custom Values and SMS/email templates for broken merge tags, `ghl_phone_wallet_audit` reads LC Phone numbers, number pools, forwarding loops and unassigned monthly wallet drain, `ghl_user_role_governance` audits staff permissions against least-privilege presets for va-support, sales-rep, client-admin and agency-admin, and `ghl_domain_ssl_onboard` plans, verifies and attaches custom funnel domains with SSL checks. The control that makes the writes safe is `ghl_agency_tools_enable`, which sets a **Safe-Writes Policy preset** of read-only, dry-run-first or standard for the session. A catalogue that repeated the card's number would have been wrong about this server for months; the entry records the measured count and says why.

[Requstory MCP](/hermes/mcp/servers/external/requstory-mcp/) is the only fully drivable surface of the window and the most operator-shaped find. `https://requstory.com/mcp` returned **HTTP 200** on a bare `initialize`, `serverInfo` `requstory` 1.0.0 on protocol `2025-06-18`, and `tools/list` returned **ten tools**. Three need no key at all: `search_companies`, `get_company_score` and `check_ai_readiness`. The score is out of 100 across four parts, find and understand, get started online, get help, and AI-agent readiness. `map_website` takes **up to six pages of a public site** and returns the customer journey, the teams with their owners, the processes and the handoffs between them, and then names the teams with **no owner** plus the checks peers pass and this company fails, which is the difference between a summary and a finding. `get_ai_company` builds a made-up company run end to end by agents from real public maps, teams as agent lanes, which is the clearest explanation of agent-native operations this catalogue has carried.

It is catalogued with its boundary stated: this is the framework and document layer, and it does not read cloud configuration to prove a control operated. **Nizh** serves frameworks to agents with posture checks and hash-chained attestations, **Fallax** turns phishing simulation results into ISO 27001 A.6.3 evidence, and **Auditspark** covers audit workflow, so the split is that the ISMS of record lives here and the technical evidence still comes from the systems.

## FAQ

### Why catalogue HighLevel Agency MCP when the card said 481 tools and the endpoint served 13?

Because thirteen measured tools are better evidence than four hundred and eighty-one claimed ones, and the thirteen are a coherent product rather than a slice of one. The card describes an API proxy. The endpoint exposes a curated set of agency operations tools behind a Safe-Writes Policy, and its audits alone, snapshot drift, merge tags, phone wallet drain and staff roles, answer questions an agency actually has. The count discrepancy is recorded in the entry so nobody later "corrects" it back to 481.

### Is a stable counter with a churning page a healthy directory?

Not on its own. A counter that does not move while pages one to three turn over completely means the index is re-ranking and re-slugging what it already holds, not ingesting. It also means page-based breadth sweeps return composition noise rather than candidates at short gaps, which is why this window's delta came entirely from the feed and the submission window.

### What makes Requstory different from the company-intelligence servers already catalogued?

It reads the process rather than the profile. Most company data servers return firmographics, funding, headcount and tech stack. Requstory returns journey stages, teams with owners, handoffs, the teams with no owner and the checks peers pass that this company fails, then scores whether an agent could actually buy from the site. That is an operations read, and `get_ai_company` extends it into what the same company looks like run by agent lanes.

### Why hold Breakwire after driving it to a full 16-tool list?

Because a well-built server and a catalogue entry are different decisions. Breakwire's breaking-change registry is unusually disciplined, source-linked claims with affected versions, deadlines, verification status and an outcome-report loop, and it was driven keyless to completion. It is also developer tooling, and this window already carried five developer tools. Driving a surface and cataloguing it are separate judgements, and saying which one happened is the point of this section.
