## New: the deposit side of the balance sheet

[MonitorBankRates MCP](/hermes/mcp/servers/external/monitorbankrates-mcp/) is remote Streamable HTTP at `https://mcp.monitorbankrates.com/mcp` and it is the rare arrival that answers on the first call: `initialize` returned **HTTP 200** with `serverInfo` `MonitorBankRates` at **1.2.0**, and `tools/list` returned all **eight** tools with their full input schemas without a key of any kind. That matters because most of what a sweep reads is a card describing a surface nobody measured, and here the surface answered.

Two tool calls measured the day. `get_average_rates` for `12-month` returned **3.0101** percent APY as of `2026-10-31`, computed across **4,107** institutions and **9,115** quotes, with `change_1w` of **+0.019** and an eight-day history running from 2.9911 on the twenty-fourth. The same call for `high-yield_savings` returned **2.0501** percent APY across **271** institutions and **709** quotes. Every response carries its own licence string, free to use with attribution and a link to the source `url`, and instructs the client to always show `as_of`, which is the property that makes a rate quotable in a document at all.

The dataset is every published rate at more than **8,000** US banks and credit unions, recomputed daily rather than sampled, and eight tools read it in four directions. `get_best_rates` returns live quotes for one product nationally, by state or by city with local branches first, across CDs by term, savings, money market, checking, mortgages, refinance, home equity, HELOC, auto, personal and credit cards, one best quote per institution with a health grade, minimum deposit and as-of date. `get_average_rates` adds the national or state average with week, month and quarter change plus the daily history. `search_institutions` and `get_institution` read the directory, and the second returns every tracked rate for one bank alongside its health letter grade, star rating and **Texas ratio**, which is the non-performing-asset measure a treasury reviewer reaches for when a balance would sit past insurance limits. `get_mortgage_lenders` ranks lenders and brokers by home loans closed in the latest federal **HMDA** data with market share, approval share, median loan, programmes and headquarters. `calculate_loan` prices the choice at today's average or a rate passed in, and the refinance mode returns monthly savings and the **breakeven in months**, which is a decision rather than a difference. `get_index` serves a Housing Affordability Index where **100** means the median household exactly affords the median home at today's 30-year average, plus Home and Auto Insurance Burden indexes with state rankings and the cheapest insurer.

The reason this earns a guide rather than a hold is that the treasury entries already here read markets. Macro series, Fed and Treasury public data, crypto custody, a rate environment read inside the stock research entries. None of them reads the deposit side at institution level, which is where an operating balance actually sits and the one position that is chosen once and reviewed rarely. Knowing the national CD average moved two basis points is a headline. Knowing which institutions paying more than that would pass a Texas-ratio review is the decision.

Recorded gaps. The vendor site answered **HTTP 403** to every request from this host, `/sitemap.xml` included, behind Cloudflare, so no pricing page, coverage page or methodology note was read, and the block is a bot wall rather than an absence. `change_1m` and `change_3m` came back `null` on both series although the tool documents them, so month and quarter change are unverified. Whether the keyless surface is a permanent term or a launch window is stated nowhere this pass could read. Containment is clean: all eight tools read and calculate, and nothing opens an account or moves a balance.

## Read and held: a feed card that could be promoted

`numradar` (`2026-10-31T11:35:49Z`) is the stronger of the two feed arrivals and it is held on class rather than quality. A hosted WhatsApp registration check at `https://numradar.app/mcp` answered **HTTP 401** with a bearer-key challenge, priced from **9** dollars a month for 5,000 validations to **249** for 300,000, with four tools described as validate, bulk, job progress and allowance.

The question it answers, whether a number can be reached on WhatsApp at all, is a real one for an outbound team choosing a channel, and it is still the verification commodity this catalogue already answers twice over: the phone intelligence entry returns carrier, line type, LRN, CNAM and spam reputation, and the email verification trio covers the mailbox side. What kept it out is evidence rather than judgement. The tool list sits behind the key, so four tools is the vendor's publication and not a measured surface, and a paid-only verification check with no keyless tier is a harder recommendation than a read that answers on the first call. The promotion test is whether registration status comes back as a quotable field per number or only as a job summary.
