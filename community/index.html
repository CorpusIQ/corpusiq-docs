    Community¶

    CorpusIQ development happens in the open. If you want a new connector, hit a bug, have a question, or just want to see what's coming — this is where that conversation lives.

    Everything runs on GitHub Discussions in this repo. No separate signup, no support portal, no ticket queue. If you have a GitHub account, you can post.

    Where to put what¶

    We use a few categories, and they each do a specific job. Picking the right one means your post gets seen by the right person faster.

    💡 Ideas — "I wish CorpusIQ could…"¶

    This is the one to use for early feature and connector ideas. Want us to add a connector for a tool you use? Want a new metric, a new prompt, a different behavior? Start with an Idea when you want discussion, upvotes, or feedback before the team scopes the work.

    If you already have a specific connector enhancement with a clear use case, current workaround, and business impact, open a tracked Connector Enhancement Request instead. That keeps concrete requests aligned with the issue workflow in CONTRIBUTING.md.

    Ideas support upvotes. If you see an existing Idea that matches what you want, upvote it instead of opening a duplicate — that's the single most useful thing you can do. Upvotes are one of the real inputs into what we build next (see the Roadmap for how priorities get set).

    Before you post: skim the Roadmap first. The thing you want might already be in flight.

    🙋 Q&A — "How do I…" / "Why is this…"¶

    Use this for questions and help. Setup trouble, a connector behaving strangely, a prompt that won't return what you expect, "is this supposed to work this way" — all Q&A.

    Q&A posts can be marked with an accepted answer, so the next person with the same question finds it fast. Before posting, the Troubleshooting guide covers the common cases.

    We watch Q&A and answer inbound questions here. If your question is account- or billing-specific (something you'd rather not post publicly), email support@corpusiq.io instead.

    📣 Announcements — "Here's what changed"¶

    This is a read-only, maintainers-only category. New connectors, notable releases, and anything you should know about lands here.

    You can't post in Announcements, but you can — and should — subscribe to it if you want to know when things ship. It's the lowest-noise way to follow CorpusIQ. The Changelog is the written record of the same information.

    General, Show and tell, Polls¶

    • General — anything that doesn't fit the above. Conversations, feedback, "has anyone else…" threads.
    • Show and tell — built something useful on top of CorpusIQ? A killer prompt, a workflow, a dashboard? Share it here. We love these.
    • Polls — occasionally we'll ask the community to weigh in on a direction with a poll. Watch for them.

    A quick map¶

    You want to… Go to
    Discuss an early connector or feature idea Ideas 💡
    Submit a specific connector enhancement Connector Enhancement Request
    Upvote something you also want Ideas 💡
    Ask how to do something / report a bug Q&A 🙋
    Find out when something ships Announcements 📣 (subscribe)
    Share something you built Show and tell
    Something private / billing email support@corpusiq.io

    How we handle what you post¶

    We read all of it. Ideas with traction get pulled into roadmap planning. Q&A gets answered. We can't promise every request gets built — the Roadmap is honest about what's prioritized, planned, and parked — but nothing posted here disappears into a void.

    Announcements and the public-facing release voice are handled by our team directly. The build and support conversation — Ideas and Q&A — is where you'll get the fastest engagement.
