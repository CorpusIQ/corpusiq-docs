# CorpusIQ vs Windsor.ai: Marketing Data vs the Whole Business

Both products connect business systems to AI. The difference is scope. Windsor.ai focuses on marketing and advertising data. CorpusIQ connects the entire business stack, finance, ecommerce, CRM, email, and analytics included, so AI can answer questions that span departments.

## What Windsor.ai does

Windsor.ai syncs marketing data to AI assistants and reporting destinations. It is strong on advertising platforms: Google Ads, Meta, TikTok. If your question is marketing-only and your data lives in ad accounts, it is a reasonable tool.

## What CorpusIQ does differently

CorpusIQ is a business data layer, not a marketing connector. One secure, read-only connection gives ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity access to your real systems, with answers cited back to the source.

## The key difference

Windsor.ai answers marketing questions. CorpusIQ answers business questions, across finance, operations, sales, marketing, and ecommerce at the same time.

Take one question: what is our true profit after ad spend, fees, returns, and cost of goods? That answer needs QuickBooks plus Shopify plus Google Ads plus Meta together. CorpusIQ handles it in one question, with every number traceable to its source system.

## Which should you choose?

- Choose Windsor.ai if every question you have is a marketing question.
- Choose CorpusIQ if your questions cross departments, or if you want one connection that covers the whole stack, with read-only live retrieval that does not retain raw customer files or full connector response payloads.
